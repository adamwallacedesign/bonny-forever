Our Story & Inspiration

Wild Scottish roots, timeless botanical design.

Founded by Nina in West Sussex, Bonny Forever was born from a deep reverence for nature and a desire to eliminate the heartbreaking waste of single-use event flowers.

The name "Bonny" is the Scottish word for beautiful, spirited, and lovely — inspired by over two decades of exploring the wild, untamed landscapes of Skye, the West Highlands, and the Outer Hebrides. We weave that poetic, organic movement directly into our floral architecture.

Every single stem is hand-selected for botanical authenticity — ultra-realistic petals, textured greenery, and natural movement that will never wilt in the summer heat or end up in a skip at midnight.

Why Faux Florals?

The sustainable choice for modern celebrations.

Traditional wedding flowers are flown thousands of miles in cold-chain freight, soaked in pesticides, and discarded into landfill after just six hours.

Zero Single-Use Waste

Our circular rental model means every premium stem is enjoyed across dozens of events, drastically reducing environmental impact.

No Water & No Chemicals

Zero water waste, zero pesticide runoff, and no refrigerated transport required on the morning of your event.

Flawless All Day Long
